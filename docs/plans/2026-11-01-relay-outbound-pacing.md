# Per-connection Relay Outbound Pacing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagent-driven development for the independent budget, transport and repository tasks. Root owns all builds and runtime tests.

**Goal:** Deliver valid large uploads without exceeding the existing relay budget or dispatching revoked work.

**Architecture:** One connection-owned FIFO accounts for every outgoing frame. Guarded requests wait before sequence allocation, then validate and enter the possible-write boundary in one caller-context turn.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, coroutines, Compose, OkHttp WebSocket, the existing encrypted protocol and unmodified Node relay.

**Spec:** [Paged editor contract](2026-11-01-paged-editor-contract.md). The observed relay failure and the invariants below are the implementation amendment.

## Global constraints

- Preserve the relay's 32-message/1-MiB one-second limit and all existing protocol frame limits.
- Keep public paged capability and production opt-in disabled; preserve native presence ownership.
- Use 1,100 ms, 24 frames and 768 KiB for the connection's conservative local rolling budget.
- Never consume a sequence before admission or replay a potentially delivered mutation.
- Preserve full drafts and actual-dispatch receipt semantics; diagnostics contain no payloads or credentials.

## Review focus

- Cancellation before enqueue, during budget wait and after claiming a write must have distinct safe outcomes.
- ACK saturation must retain the highest cursor without blocking the reader or exhausting reserved control space.
- Source replacement, selection changes and connection replacement must reject the appropriate queued authority.
- Startup frames, ordinary frames and automatic ACKs must share accounting without sequence gaps.
- Main-thread and network buffering delays remain measured qualification limits, not guarantees from local timestamps.

## Observed failure

In the Native production-chain fixture, Android received upload-begin and eleven full-chunk acknowledgements. It sent the twelfth chunk, at offset 540,672, 872 ms after the first full chunk; the relay closed that peer with code 1000. The instrumented relay identified its inbound rate-limit branch: more than 1 MiB or 32 phone frames within its one-second window. Each 49,152-byte chunk becomes 65,536 base64url characters before JSON, encryption and a second base64url encoding. The empty-final RPC case does not send chunks. No owner commit was dispatched in the failed Native run.

This plan preserves the relay's limits, upload chunk contract and Native presence ownership. It addresses all outbound peer traffic on a connection, not only editor chunks: an isolated chunk delay cannot account for concurrent requests, acknowledgements or presence.

## Design and invariants

- Put a per-connection scheduler/budget at the existing serialized outbound transport lane. Account for **every** peer frame crossing that connection, including handshake, ordinary commands, attachment chunks, presence and automatic ACKs. Use conservative byte and message headroom below 1 MiB and 32 frames over a monotonic rolling interval; include the complete serialized encrypted WebSocket frame, not decoded bytes or plaintext alone. A rolling window is stricter than any aligned one-second fixed window on the sender's clock. Because queueing and network jitter can bunch arrivals, headroom and real relay verification remain required; do not claim a mathematical arrival-time guarantee from the sender clock.
- Preserve connection-specific FIFO/causal ordering without priority reordering. Bound the pending queue to 64 items and 512 KiB of estimated frame cost; reserve eight item slots and 16 KiB of that capacity for ACK and presence. Ordinary synchronous `send` throws on overflow without closing the connection or draining other queued items; guarded admission is cancellable before the possible-write callback. ACK admission is a separate nonblocking, nonthrowing path, including when the reserve is exhausted. Disconnect cancels waiting work, releases reservations and creates a fresh connection budget. The configured queue and rolling budget imply about 4.4 seconds of maximum **local model** backlog; this excludes arbitrary Main scheduling and network delay and must be measured rather than treated as an actual delivery-time guarantee.
- An ACK cursor advances and `unackedChars` resets only when its ACK is actually admitted or safely coalesced into an unsent ACK at the FIFO tail. On a full queue retain the highest pending cursor; retry on released capacity or by a single coalesced timer without spinning. Coalescing cannot cross any queued frame and never drops a cursor. Capacity callbacks run outside queue locks; never hold a queue or ACK lock while waiting for budget, Main or socket work. Test more than eight concurrent control admissions, mixed traffic, full-queue drain and connection close, ensuring the reader is never failed by ACK saturation and the highest accepted cursor is eventually sent when capacity returns.
- Wait for sufficient budget **before** consuming a secure-channel sequence number. Cost can be bounded from actual serialized plaintext length, GCM tag and base64url/JSON envelope; verify the exact frame length against that reservation when sealing. Never seal and silently discard a frame that advanced sequence. Count actual writes and conservative reservations so concurrent traffic cannot overdraw a shared window.
- A delayed editor command must be revocable while waiting. Capture its exact question, owner/source/grant, session, connection and epoch; immediately before a possible write revalidate on the repository's confined thread or use a thread-safe cancellation/dispatch gate whose invalidation and dispatch are serialized. A plain worker-thread snapshot of UI ownership is insufficient. If validation fails, skip without sending and preserve draft/cancel behavior. All delayed paths must honor disconnect, background, replacement, cancellation and current authority; no independent background presence.
- Preserve the meaning of `request`'s `onSent`: it runs at the possible-write boundary, before any write that might have reached Host, including throwing or uncertain sends. A command cancelled before that boundary must not create an uncertain-commit receipt; once a write may have begun it must retain the receipt and must never auto-retry. The repository request timeout starts only after pacing admission, not while awaiting budget. The scheduler/API design must explicitly resolve coordination between main-confined authority, serialized transport and socket send without creating an unchecked gap. A conservative earlier receipt is safe against duplicate writes but can incorrectly quarantine an upload that provably never dispatched, so test that distinction.
- `RemoteTransport` adds `suspend fun sendGuarded(payload: JsonObject, beforePossibleWrite: () -> Unit)`. Its default implementation checks caller cancellation, invokes the callback, then delegates to existing `send`, preserving fake-transport compatibility. The real transport captures caller context and Job and queues a guarded deferred into the **same** FIFO as ordinary sends and ACKs. It waits for budget before sealing, then uses `withContext(callerContext.minusKey(Job))` for a non-suspending caller-context turn: atomically claim the cancellation gate, verify the captured caller Job remains active and the connection/generation remains current and active, invoke `beforePossibleWrite`, then seal and call `WebSocket.send`. The sender awaits the turn before the next frame. If cancellation wins before the claim, no callback, seal or write occurs; after claim/write, keep the outcome conservative and never automatically replay. Ensure the claim and cancellation are a single CAS state transition and document how cancellation after claim is reported.
- Every repository `request()` uses guarded dispatch. Its callback rechecks `checkEpoch(epoch)`, connected state and captured connection generation, then runs the existing `onSent`. Paged upload's per-command callback additionally checks the full captured `valid()` predicate. Set `commitDispatched` and add its receipt only from the commit's actual `onSent` callback; use it rather than merely reaching the commit branch to decide whether a failure is outcome-unknown. Start response timeout after guarded dispatch completes. `awaitConnectionReply` also uses guarded dispatch, but validates the captured **connection generation and route**, not selection epoch: navigating within the same connection must not cancel a connection-bound request; replacing the connection must prevent its write. Its timeout likewise starts after dispatch. Direct ordinary `send` and ACKs share the paced FIFO, without a repository callback.
- The actual rolling budget is **1,100 ms, 24 frames, 768 KiB**. Estimate an encrypted frame conservatively from serialized UTF-8 plaintext bytes, a 16-byte GCM tag, base64url expansion and 1,024 bytes of envelope allowance. Test that actual serialized frame length never exceeds the estimate, then record the **actual** length when the socket send is attempted. All startup `hello`/`sendNow` and pairing/user handshake paths participate in the same ledger: check immediately and fail closed before sealing when a frame would not fit; do not bypass the budget. The guarded main-context turn performs bounded crypto and socket enqueue on the caller thread; measure its latency and heartbeat impact on a real device. Verify cancellation/teardown cannot deadlock the sender. This API and its fake semantics still require independent review before implementation.
- No plaintext, credentials, grant IDs or document bytes in diagnostics. Maintain existing strict chunk sizes, acknowledgements, SHA-256, frame caps and exact error semantics.

## Work and verification gates

1. **Pure budget model.** Add `src/main/java/de/joinnoah/pi/remote/OutboundPacing.kt`: `internal class OutboundFrameBudget` with `fun delayMillis(nowMillis: Long, estimatedBytes: Int): Long`, `fun record(nowMillis: Long, actualBytes: Int)`, `fun reset()`, and companion `fun encryptedFrameUpperBound(plaintextBytes: Int): Int`; it owns the 1,100 ms/24/768 KiB rolling ledger and rejects impossible/negative sizes. The single serialized sender owns this ledger; no concurrent reservations outside it. Add `internal class OutboundQueueBudget` with `fun tryReserve(estimatedBytes: Int, control: Boolean): Boolean`, `fun release(estimatedBytes: Int, control: Boolean)` and `fun reset()`, enforcing 64 items/512 KiB with eight slots/16 KiB reserved for ACK/presence. Transport serializes queue-budget access and pairs every successful reserve with exactly one release. Add `src/test/java/de/joinnoah/pi/remote/OutboundPacingTest.kt`; test rolling boundaries, exact limits and impossible frames, conservative encoding estimates, byte/message/slot control reserve, queue cancellation/release/overflow, reconnect reset, FIFO integration, max wait and headroom. Test bursty control and uploads together, not just a single uploader. The model does not itself prove remote arrival timing.
2. **Transport integration.** Design and review the API for asynchronous admission, captured cancellation, authority validation and possible-write notifications before implementation. Exercise the real encrypted sender with a controllable clock/socket: measure serialized frame lengths and send times, count all message types including handshake, test ACK exhaustion/coalescing/retry and safe queue-lock ordering, show no new sequence gap or leaked reservation, preserve ordering, and prove no stale queued upload is sent after disconnect/replacement/revocation. Test cancellation both before possible write and after write begins, including commit receipt and no retry. Preserve the lease's visible-chat lifecycle and regular presence under sustained upload.
3. **Repository and real chain.** Adapt upload and connection-bound requests to the reviewed API; revalidate captured editor state after every suspension and before dispatch. Test connection-bound requests survive selection change but never write to a replaced connection. Verify cancel wins before write, no stale grant transfer, and unknown outcome after a potentially delivered commit. Run fresh private Native and managed RPC 1 MiB cases through the unmodified actual relay, under concurrent control traffic: record relay window decisions, real frame sizes/timing, every chunk/ACK, matching Host/owner ACK and SDK digest, active presence, no disconnect and exact cleanup. Retest empty final, cancellation, replacement and deliberately lost ACK. Keep physical-device, network-variability, independent Linux and broader matrix qualification separate. Root owns builds and private emulator/relay processes.

Before implementation, independently review how the scheduler can enforce the authority and sequence invariants against the existing synchronous transport API; do not paper over them with a local upload-only delay.
